## Ask the NCC — free

**The text of the National Construction Code 2025, in plain English, on your phone.**

Ask a question the way you'd ask a mate on site — *"how far does the shed have to be from the house?"* — and get the answer with the clause number and a link to it on the ABCB's site. Volumes One, Two and Three and the Housing Provisions are built in. Nothing to upload.

- **Your state's rules.** Set WA, NSW, QLD… once and the state variations come through automatically.
- **Every answer shows its source.** Tap the clause to read it on ncc.abcb.gov.au.
- **Free account: 3 questions a day.** Go Pro to add your own standards and ask as much as you like.

[Ask the NCC — free →](https://app.standaid.ai/auth)
